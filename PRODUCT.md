# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Wear OS enthusiasts and design-conscious smartwatch owners looking for distinctive, niche alternatives to mass-market watch faces. Visitors may arrive from Google Play, community recommendations, or developer-profile verification pages and should quickly understand who Demand Group is and what it creates.

## Product Purpose

Present Demand Group as a small independent Wear OS watch-face studio, showcase its distinctive work, direct visitors to its Google Play releases, and provide a credible official web presence suitable for Google organization-account verification.

## Brand Personality

Premium, vintage, eccentric, selective, and collectible. The voice is confident and concise, with the character of an independent instrument atelier rather than a large software marketplace.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic corporate technology sites, neon sci-fi aesthetics, crowded subscription catalogs, fake luxury claims, imitation traditional-watch branding, excessive feature grids, and inflated marketing language.

## Design Principles

1. Treat every watch face as a designed object, not a catalog entry.
2. Let a small, carefully selected collection communicate confidence and taste.
3. Combine vintage instrument character with contemporary digital clarity.
4. Make the independent nature of the studio visible and credible.
5. Keep the path to Google Play, support, and organization information immediate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and interaction standards. Provide keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, semantic structure, descriptive alternative text, comfortable mobile reading, and reduced-motion behavior for visitors who request it.
