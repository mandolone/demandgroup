Demand Group / Promotion privacy

Your email, with a clear purpose.

Last updated: 30 October 2026. This notice explains the private partner promotion and the separate, optional monthly-updates list.

Who handles your information

The data controller is Luca Mandolesi, operating as Demand Group. Contact: demand.group@tuta.io. You can use this email for questions about this notice and to exercise your data-protection rights.

What we collect and why

Your email address and verification records are processed through Supabase Auth to verify your identity, deliver access without a password and let you recover an assigned code. The campaign database keeps a keyed pseudonymous email identifier, account identifier, chosen watch face, partner attribution, assignment date and code. This is necessary to administer your requested promotional benefit and enforce the campaign limit.

Short-lived keyed IP digests and request counts help prevent automated or repeated requests. Your IP is not used as proof of identity or to enforce one code per household. The system does not use device fingerprinting. Approximate catalog page views use a random request identifier to avoid counting retries twice; no visit identifier is stored in your browser. These counts do not measure individual people.

Receiving the benefit does not subscribe you to a mailing list. Marketing emails are sent only if you separately choose the optional, unchecked monthly-updates box and verify your email. Refusing or withdrawing this consent does not affect your promotional code. Essential authentication data is stored only for this browser tab and can be removed by signing out or closing the tab.

Service providers and redemption

Supabase provides authentication and the campaign database. The project’s primary database region is West EU — Ireland (eu-west-1). Resend sends verification emails and processes the recipient address, message and delivery information. Cloudflare Turnstile provides bot protection and processes browser security signals. GitHub Pages hosts the public site and processes ordinary access logs under its own policies. Partners receive the shared invitation QR; they do not receive your email list or direct access to individual assignment records.

An EU database region does not mean that every provider operation stays in the EU: support, email delivery and security processing can involve other countries and subprocessors. The providers’ processing terms include safeguards for applicable international transfers, such as Standard Contractual Clauses. You can request details or copies of applicable safeguards from us. Read the Supabase processing terms, Resend processing terms, Cloudflare processing terms and their privacy policies: Supabase, Resend, Cloudflare and GitHub.

If you open a Google Play redemption link, your assigned code is sent to Google. Google manages redemption, compatibility and installation under its own terms and privacy policy. Demand Group records assignment; it does not claim to know whether you redeemed or installed the watch face.

Private partner invitations

The promotion requires a private partner QR link. Its access key is validated server-side and stored only as a hash in the campaign database. The key identifies a shared partner invitation, not an individual person or physical presence at a venue. It is not stored in browser storage or sent to the static host as part of the page request. Do not forward it publicly. It may be replaced, revoked or expire independently of your assigned code.

Optional monthly updates

If you choose monthly updates, we store your verified email, verification time, consent date, exact consent wording/version and source partner. This separate purpose relies on your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. We send at most one update per month about new Demand Group watch faces. You can withdraw consent in the email-preferences section after verifying your email, use the unsubscribe link in every newsletter, or contact us. The newsletter has not launched. The administrator can export only opted-in, verified contacts. A delivery provider has not yet been selected or connected; its identity, processor arrangements and applicable transfer safeguards must be communicated before any transfer or newsletter send.

Newsletter consent expires after 24 months unless you explicitly renew it; you can unsubscribe earlier. Withdrawn addresses are excluded from new subscriber exports immediately and are kept for up to 30 days solely to synchronize removal with the mailing provider. Minimal pseudonymous consent evidence is kept for up to 24 months after withdrawal. Newsletter records are separate from the promotional campaign’s retention period. Before any future send, the operator must apply site withdrawals in the selected delivery service and respect its unsubscribe list; the systems are not automatically synchronized.

Legal basis and retention

We verify your email, assign your chosen code and allow recovery to fulfil the promotional benefit you request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Proportionate security checks, rate limits and the minimal record used to prevent repeat gifts rely on our legitimate interest in protecting limited promotional inventory, keeping the service secure and applying the same eligibility rule to all participants (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You may object to processing based on legitimate interests; we will consider your circumstances. Optional marketing consent is separate and can be withdrawn at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing. We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects solely through automated processing; allocation is limited to the promotional eligibility and stock rules described here.

The partner program can remain open without a scheduled end date. For continuing programs, a daily cleanup removes non-administrator Auth accounts and their direct campaign account linkage after 90 days without campaign use. Accounts created but never used for the campaign are removed after 90 days without a sign-in. Administrator accounts remain while required to operate the program and are reviewed separately. A minimal keyed email identifier and assignment record is retained while the partner program continues, including temporary pauses, to enforce the single-gift rule and enable recovery after renewed email verification. These keyed records are pseudonymous personal data, not anonymous data. Once the program closes, campaign identity and assignment links are removed after the configured retention period, initially 90 days. Returning does not entitle a recipient to another gift. Anonymous inventory tombstones and aggregate distribution totals may remain to prevent code reuse. Raw visit events are kept for 30 days, random visit deduplication records for up to 3 days, and rate-limit records for at most 48 hours after expiry. Real Google Play codes retain their own expiry even when the partner program has no scheduled end. We review whether the continuing program and its minimal records are still needed; permanent closure triggers the retention period above. We publish changes to these purposes or retention rules before applying them. These retention periods are cleanup thresholds; removal can take one additional daily cleanup cycle. Provider security and delivery logs and backups follow their documented retention schedules; removal from the live campaign database does not imply instantaneous removal from every provider backup.

Your choices and rights

You may choose not to verify your email; you can still browse, but cannot receive a code. Contact the address above to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or to object where applicable. You may complain to your competent data protection authority, including the Italian Garante. We normally respond to rights requests within one month, subject to the extensions permitted by GDPR. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Deletion may end code recovery; an assigned code is never returned to inventory.

One verified email is an identity signal. The service cannot guarantee that a person with several different email addresses will never make another claim.
