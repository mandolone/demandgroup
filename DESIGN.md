# Design System

## Direction

Vintage instrument atelier: contemporary clarity shaped by scientific dials, dark painted metal, aged brass, and selective collector culture.

## Theme

Dark, neutral-black foundation with committed moss olive sections and controlled brass highlights. The experience is tactile and cinematic without sci-fi glow or imitation luxury styling.

## Color

- Background: `oklch(0.075 0 0)`
- Surface: `oklch(0.125 0.012 120)`
- Ink: `oklch(0.96 0.006 90)`
- Muted: `oklch(0.7 0.018 105)`
- Primary moss: `oklch(0.38 0.08 120)`
- Brass accent: `oklch(0.73 0.115 78)`

## Typography

Barlow Condensed for expressive display typography and Manrope for readable body copy. Headlines stay below 96px and use restrained negative tracking.

## Layout

Single-page brand narrative with one decisive hero, asymmetric editorial statements, a structured collection register, compatibility information, contact, and privacy. Spacious desktop rhythm collapses to a direct single-column mobile flow.

## Interaction

Purposeful short transitions, visible keyboard focus, semantic anchors, and an explicit reduced-motion fallback.

## Imagery

Premium product still life on matte black with moss and aged-brass instrument details. Current hero is concept imagery and is labelled accordingly until real product captures replace it.
