Will this work on my smartwatch?

A compatible Wear OS smartwatch is required. Apple Watch is not supported. Check the individual Google Play listing using your Google account and device: requirements can differ between watch faces.

How do I install a watch face?

Open the model’s Google Play page and follow the installation options available for your compatible watch. If you use its phone companion, keep your watch connected, use “Install on watch”, then complete the installation in Google Play on the watch. You may need to select the new face in your watch’s face selector afterwards.

Why does Google Play show my phone as incompatible?

The watch face requires a compatible Wear OS watch. Check whether Google Play offers your watch as an installation target. If you cannot find an option, email us with the model name, watch model and the message you see.

Where can I check the price?

R4 Star, R5S and OUTFIT are free. Other released models are paid. Google Play displays the current price for your country and account before purchase.

How do I get support?

Email demandgroup@tuta.io with the watch face name, watch model and a description of the issue. Never send passwords or payment details.
