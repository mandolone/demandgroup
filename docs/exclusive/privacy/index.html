Who handles your information

The data controller is Luca Mandolesi, operating as Demand Group. Contact: demand.group@tuta.io. The controller’s postal contact details are held in the private review draft and will be finalized before launch.

What we collect and why

Your email address and verification records are processed through Supabase Auth to verify your identity, deliver access without a password and let you recover an assigned code. The campaign database keeps a keyed pseudonymous email identifier, account identifier, chosen watch face, partner attribution, assignment date and code. This is necessary to administer your requested promotional benefit and enforce the campaign limit.

Short-lived keyed IP digests and request counts help prevent automated or repeated requests. Your IP is not used as proof of identity or to enforce one code per household. The system does not use device fingerprinting. Approximate catalog page views use a random request identifier to avoid counting retries twice; no visit identifier is stored in your browser. These counts do not measure individual people.

Receiving the benefit does not subscribe you to a mailing list. Marketing emails are sent only if you separately choose the optional, unchecked monthly-updates box and verify your email. Refusing or withdrawing this consent does not affect your promotional code. Essential authentication data is stored only for this browser tab and can be removed by signing out or closing the tab.

Service providers and redemption

Supabase provides authentication and the campaign database; the operator must document the selected hosting region, processing agreement and any international transfer safeguards. Cloudflare Turnstile provides bot protection and receives browser security signals. Read Cloudflare’s privacy policy and Supabase’s privacy policy. GitHub hosts the public site and may process ordinary access logs under its own policies.

If you open a Google Play redemption link, your assigned code is sent to Google. Google manages redemption, compatibility and installation under its own terms and privacy policy. Demand Group records assignment; it does not claim to know whether you redeemed or installed the watch face.

Private partner invitations

The promotion requires a private partner QR link. Its access key is validated server-side and stored only as a hash in the campaign database. The key identifies a shared partner invitation, not an individual person or physical presence at a venue. It is not stored in browser storage or sent to the static host as part of the page request. Do not forward it publicly. It may be replaced, revoked or expire independently of your assigned code.

Optional monthly updates

If you choose monthly updates, we store your verified email, verification time, consent date, exact consent wording/version and source partner. This separate purpose relies on your consent. We send at most one update per month about new Demand Group watch faces. You can withdraw consent in the email-preferences section after verifying your email, use the unsubscribe link in every newsletter, or contact us. The newsletter has not launched. The administrator can export only opted-in, verified contacts. A delivery provider has not yet been selected or connected; its identity, processor arrangements and applicable transfer safeguards must be communicated before any transfer or newsletter send.

Newsletter consent expires after 24 months unless you explicitly renew it; you can unsubscribe earlier. Withdrawn addresses are excluded from new subscriber exports immediately and are kept for up to 30 days solely to synchronize removal with the mailing provider. Minimal pseudonymous consent evidence is kept for up to 24 months after withdrawal. Newsletter records are separate from the promotional campaign’s retention period. Before any future send, the operator must apply site withdrawals in the selected delivery service and respect its unsubscribe list; the systems are not automatically synchronized.

Legal basis and retention

The proposed purposes are administering the promotional benefit you request and proportionate fraud prevention. Before launch the controller must confirm the applicable legal bases and communicate them here, including any legitimate interest relied on.

The partner program can remain open without a scheduled end date. For continuing programs, the prepared default removes non-administrator Auth accounts and their direct campaign account linkage after 90 days without authenticated activity, using a scheduled cleanup. A minimal keyed email identifier and assignment record is retained while the partner program continues, including temporary pauses, to enforce the single-gift rule and enable recovery after renewed email verification. These keyed records are pseudonymous personal data, not anonymous data. Once the program closes, campaign identity and assignment links are removed after the configured retention period, initially 90 days. Returning does not entitle a recipient to another gift. Anonymous inventory tombstones and aggregate distribution totals may remain to prevent code reuse. Raw visit events are kept for 30 days, random visit deduplication records for up to 3 days, and rate-limit records for at most 48 hours after expiry. Real Google Play codes retain their own expiry even when the partner program has no scheduled end. The controller must review the ongoing need for these records and publish changes before applying them.

Your choices and rights

You may choose not to verify your email; you can still browse, but cannot receive a code. Contact the address above to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or to object where applicable. You may complain to your competent data protection authority, including the Italian Garante. Deletion may end code recovery; an assigned code is never returned to inventory.

One verified email is an identity signal. The service cannot guarantee that a person with several different email addresses will never make another claim.
