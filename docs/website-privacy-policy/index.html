Website Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

This notice describes the processing of personal data connected with the Demand Group presentation website, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and applicable Italian privacy law.

Data controller

The controller is Demand Group, an independent studio based in Italy. Privacy enquiries may be sent to demandgroup@tuta.io.

Data processed

The website does not provide user accounts, contact forms, advertising, behavioural profiling or audience analytics. Hosting and network providers may automatically process technical connection data such as IP address, requested resource, date and time, browser information and security logs. Demand Group does not use this information to identify or profile visitors.

If you contact us by email, we process your address, message and information you provide only to answer and manage the correspondence.

Purposes and legal bases

  • Delivering, securing and maintaining the website: legitimate interests and, where applicable, steps requested by the visitor.
  • Answering emails and business enquiries: steps requested by the sender and legitimate interests in managing communications.
  • Complying with legal obligations or defending legal claims where necessary.

Recipients and international transfers

The website is published through GitHub Pages. Technical data may therefore be processed by GitHub and its service providers under their own privacy terms and appropriate transfer mechanisms. Demand Group does not sell personal data.

Retention

Demand Group does not maintain a visitor database. Technical logs are retained by hosting providers under their policies. Email correspondence is kept only as long as reasonably necessary.

Your rights

Subject to GDPR conditions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or objection, and withdraw consent at any time. Contact demandgroup@tuta.io. You may also complain to the Italian Data Protection Authority.
