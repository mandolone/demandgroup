Kanji Wave Japanese Watch Face

Read time in kanji. Let the waves carry the day.

Waves frame Mount Fuji in a landscape inspired by ukiyo-e, with echoes of Hokusai and Hiroshige. Red kanji mark the time against deep black, while three layers of parallax give the artwork depth.

  • Hours, minutes, seconds and date in Japanese
  • Foreground waves, background waves and Mount Fuji on three moving layers
  • Weather blended into the landscape
  • Subtle liquid animation showing your step-goal percentage
  • Optional time-zone reference in Arabic numerals
  • Blue, warm, purple and monochrome palettes

From Italy, with admiration for Japan

Born from our personal love of Japan and the long cultural friendship between our countries. A small tribute to the expressive power of Japanese art, the purity of its lines and the balance between detail and open space.
