Cookie Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

This policy explains cookies used on the Demand Group website. Under Italian rules, technical cookies do not require prior consent but must be disclosed. Non-technical tracking may be activated only after valid consent.

What we currently use

The site currently uses no analytics, advertising or profiling cookies. It stores only the technical preference cookie below.

Technical cookie

Name
dg_cookie_consent
Purpose
Remembers whether you selected necessary-only or accepted optional categories.
Provider
Demand Group (first party)
Duration
Up to 183 days
Legal basis
Necessary to store the user's privacy preference.

Optional categories

Analytics and marketing categories appear for transparency and future compatibility, but they are currently “Not in use” and no related script or cookie is loaded. Accepting them does not currently activate tracking.

Your choice

You may continue with necessary cookies only, accept optional categories, or close the banner without consent. Access is never restricted. Change your choice using .

Browser controls

You may inspect, block or delete cookies in browser settings. Deleting the preference cookie makes the banner appear again.

Italian summary

Il sito utilizza attualmente solo il cookie tecnico dg_cookie_consent, necessario per ricordare la scelta e conservato fino a 183 giorni. Non sono attivi cookie analytics, pubblicitari o di profilazione. È sempre possibile proseguire con i soli cookie necessari.
